What the score means

The scan-level evidence score runs from 0 to 100 and classifies each annotation as consistent evidence, inconclusive, or inconsistent evidence.

  • Structure agreement and Tanimoto structural similarity carry the most weight.
  • Inconclusive annotations may reflect isomers or competing structural assignments.
  • Entropy similarity can be enabled for additional spectral evidence.
